## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce sales dataset to identify sales trends,
profitability, regional performance, category performance, and business
opportunities.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business
insights that can support better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

•⁠  ⁠Analyze overall sales and profit performance
•⁠  ⁠Identify the best-performing categories and sub-categories
•⁠  ⁠Compare regional sales and profitability
•⁠  ⁠Analyze the relationship between discount and profit
•⁠  ⁠Identify loss-making products/sub-categories
•⁠  ⁠Generate actionable business recommendations

## 📊 Key Insights

- Total Sales: 2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: 286,397.02
- Average Sales: 229.86
- Average Profit: 28.66
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.47%

### Category Performance

- Technology generated the highest sales of 836,154.03.
- Technology also generated the highest profit of 145,454.95.
- Furniture generated good sales but comparatively lower profit.
- Office Supplies showed stable performance across regions.

### Regional Performance

- West region generated the highest overall sales.
- West region also had the highest average profit.
- Central region showed comparatively lower profitability.

### Sub-Category Performance

- Copiers generated the highest total profit of approximately 55.6K.
- Tables generated the highest loss of approximately 17.7K.
- Phones were among the top-performing sub-categories by sales.
- Tables require further analysis to improve profitability.

### Discount Analysis

- Lower discount levels generally showed better average profitability.
- Higher discounts, especially around 50%, were associated with negative average profit.
- Excessive discounting can significantly reduce profitability.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Focus more on high-performing Technology products to maintain strong sales and profitability.

2. Review the pricing, discounts, and costs associated with Tables because this sub-category is generating a significant loss.

3. Avoid excessive discounting, especially at higher discount levels, as it can negatively affect profit.

4. Analyze Furniture products separately to identify why strong sales are not converting into higher profits.

5. Continue monitoring regional performance and focus on regions with stronger profitability.

## 🚀 Conclusion

The analysis shows that Technology is the strongest category in terms of both sales and profit. However, Furniture requires improvement because its sales are relatively strong while its profitability remains comparatively low.

The analysis also highlights Tables as a major loss-making sub-category and excessive discounts as a potential factor affecting profitability.

These insights can help the business improve pricing, discount strategies, product focus, and overall profitability.
